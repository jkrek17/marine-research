How scoring works

One storm system per case, marked by a dashed ring. The analysed low and any stationary front are shown. Draw its cold, warm and occluded fronts: pick the type, tap points along the front and finish it. Submit to compare with the Unified Surface Analysis (OPC / WPC / NHC).

Fronts: each analysed front of the system in the ring (not troughs, not other systems) is matched to one of yours, one to one. Position credit is 1 within 150 nm of the analysed line, falling to 0 at 300 nm. Type credit counts twice as much and is scaled by position: exact type 1; half for occluded or stationary against cold, and stationary against warm; cold against warm 0.

Misses cost the front's share of the total analysed length, weighted up when it is near the low. Extra fronts inside the ring with no match cost a little. Fronts drawn outside the ring cost nothing.

Clock: 90 s a case. At zero, what you have drawn is submitted. Untick Clock to work without it.

Maps are GFS analyses (0.5 deg). Truth is the coded Unified Surface Analysis. Dates are hidden. Training use only.
