Peak wind guidance

What the verification shows: the model's 10 m wind is lifted 8 kt where it reaches 44 kt, and the ceiling is checked against the strongest 925 mb wind and the sea-air temperature difference. Includes the 925 mb wind each category requires and the model biases by category.

Read the guidance
Scoring and verification

Categories: none under 34 kt, gale 34-47, storm 48-63, hurricane force 64+. Truth: the strongest ASCAT 10 m wind anywhere on the map (third-highest cell, to reject single-cell noise).

Score: exact 100; over by one 50, by two or more 10; under by one 25, by two or more 0; a missed hurricane force case costs 25. Misses cost more than false alarms, as on the desk.

Timed set: 10 cases, 60 seconds each. Hints can be switched on or off during any practice session.

Data: GFS analyses, GFS and ECMWF +24 h forecasts (ECMWF 00 and 12 UTC runs from March 2025). Dates are hidden.

Suggested sequence

  1. Read the Peak wind guidance (about five minutes).
  2. Practise Analysis with hints on, then move to the 24-hour forecast.
  3. Run the Benchmark now and again later to measure progress.
Data: NOAA GFS; ECMWF open data; NOAA GOES imagery; ASCAT (Metop-B/C) from Remote Sensing Systems; OPC/WPC/NHC Unified Surface Analysis. For training only; not an official forecast product.
