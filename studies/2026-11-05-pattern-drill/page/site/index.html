New here?

  1. Read Reading the wind once (five minutes).
  2. Play Right now with hints on until the calls feel natural, then Beat the model.
  3. Run the Benchmark now and again later to see if you have improved.
  4. Mix in the Front drill for where the systems are, not just how strong.
Maps: NOAA GFS, ECMWF open data, GOES imagery. Wind truth: ASCAT (Metop-B/C) from Remote Sensing Systems. Fronts: the OPC/WPC/NHC Unified Surface Analysis. Training use only; not an official forecast product.
