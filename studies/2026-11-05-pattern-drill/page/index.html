How scoring works

Category call: exact 100. Over by one 50, by two or more 10. Under by one 25, by two or more 0. Missing an observed HF -25.

Benchmarks on every case: GFS and ECMWF (their own strongest 10 m wind) and 925 × stability (strongest 925 mb wind on the map times a mixing factor from sea minus air) are scored the same way, so the stats show whether you beat them.
